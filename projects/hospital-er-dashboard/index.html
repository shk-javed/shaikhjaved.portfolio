01 / The problem

Make the emergency room readable.

Source: README

Raw hospital emergency data hides the things an ER team needs at a glance: how many patients came in, how long they waited, how satisfied they were, who was admitted and where they were referred.

This dashboard turns that data into structured, actionable views for operational planning, patient experience and resource allocation.
